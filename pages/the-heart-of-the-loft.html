The Heart of the Loft

“Together, we are committed to raising the standard of childcare in our community, creating a center that parents can trust, children can love, and families can count on for years to come.”

The earliest years of life are the most important.

At Little Legends Learning Loft, we believe childhood is the beginning of a lifelong story, and every story deserves a strong, beautiful start. The earliest years of life are the most important. They shape how children learn, how they grow, and who they will one day become. The quality of care children receive in these years leaves a lasting impact on their confidence, their curiosity, and their ability to succeed.

We created the Loft with one clear purpose: to pour into our Legends and help build the foundation for tomorrow’s leaders. Here, children are not just cared for; they are celebrated, encouraged, and inspired every single day.

Our vision is simple yet powerful: to help raise confident, kind, and capable children who will grow into productive citizens and shining examples of leadership in their community. We know that when children feel safe, loved, and supported, they thrive, and when families feel confident in their child’s care, they too can flourish.

Meet Iris Vanblunt, Founder & Director

Iris brings passion, vision, and a powerful blend of creativity and leadership to Little Legends Learning Loft. With 15 years of experience in banking, finance, and business operations, she has developed the skills to build systems, structure, and stability. These strengths play a major role in creating the high-quality learning environment families find at the Loft.

Her background in business gave her a strong foundation in management, organization, and customer experience. Those strengths now guide everything from the Loft’s daily operations to the development of signature enrichment programs. Iris has a gift for blending structure with heart, creating a space that is not only safe and organized but also warm, joyful, and full of opportunities for children to explore, grow, and shine.

For Iris, opening the Loft is more than a business venture. It is a lifelong calling. She has spent years caring for and nurturing children within her family and community, witnessing how the right environment can shape a child’s confidence, curiosity, and future. That experience fuels her commitment to creating a place where children feel loved, supported, and empowered.

Every successful company has a great team. Little Legends Learning Loft proudly operates under McMillon Global Enterprises, the family holding company she and her partner built together. They bring strong leadership, innovation, and an unwavering commitment to excellence, creating opportunities for children, families, and communities to thrive. That quiet but powerful partnership is the steady foundation beneath everything the Loft stands for.

Under Iris’s leadership, Little Legends Learning Loft is ready to welcome your family into the village, a place where your child will be loved, celebrated, and empowered to become the legend they were born to be.

Meet Kash & Pixie

Every legend needs a friend by their side. Kash the lion cub and Pixie the ladybug are the heart of the Loft, cheering on every child as they learn, play, and grow into the leaders they were born to be.

Pixie is a heartfelt tribute to Princess Tolliver (May 22, 1991 - January 16, 2023). Her love for children, kindness, and generous heart live on through Pixie, watching over every Legend who walks through our doors.

At Little Legends, we believe it takes a village, and we are honored to be part of yours.
