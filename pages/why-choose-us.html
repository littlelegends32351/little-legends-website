Why Choose Us

Because childhood should be more than care, it should be a foundation for success.

Our Promise

At Little Legends Learning Loft, we promise to be more than just a childcare center, we are a place where children feel safe, loved, and inspired every single day. When you entrust us with your child, you are not simply choosing childcare, you are joining a family that is committed to guiding, encouraging, and celebrating your Little Legend at every step.

Safety First

From secure entry systems to emergency drills, your child’s safety is always our top priority. With WatchMeGrow cameras, you get secure weekly viewing time to check in on your little one.

Enrichment Every Day

We go beyond daycare basics. Our enrichment-based approach builds confidence, creativity, and leadership skills, preparing children not only for school, but for life.

Diversity & Inclusion

We honor all cultures, backgrounds, and unique abilities, creating a welcoming space where every child and every family belongs.

Family Partnership

With Brightwheel, you’ll receive instant updates, photos, and messages throughout the day. Our Legend Mentors don’t just supervise, they guide, nurture, and inspire.

Our Mission

To provide safe, loving, and enrichment-focused care that lays the foundation for lifelong success. We are committed to guiding, encouraging, and celebrating every child so they grow with confidence, curiosity, and kindness.

Our Vision

To be Quincy’s most trusted family-owned learning center, a place where every child feels valued, every parent feels supported, and our community grows stronger through the Legends we help raise.

Growing With Your Child

Infant Legends

6 weeks to 12 months

More than care, infants receive nurturing routines that build trust, security, and early brain development. With tummy time, sensory play, and gentle interaction, we focus on milestones like rolling, sitting, and early communication.

Toddler Legends

1 to 2 years

Our toddler program is all about discovery. Little Legends explore with busy hands and curious minds through music, movement, and hands-on activities. We help toddlers build independence, language skills, and social awareness while giving them plenty of space to play and grow safely.

Preschool Legends

3 to 4 years

Preschool is the launchpad for kindergarten success. Through pre-literacy, early math, and problem-solving activities, we guide children in developing both academic skills and emotional readiness. Creative play, art, and group learning prepare them for a smooth transition to “big kid school.”

School-Age Legends

5 to 12 years

Our before- and after-school care goes beyond homework help. We provide enrichment activities, leadership opportunities, and a safe space for friendships. From academic support to fun clubs and projects, older Legends build confidence and responsibility while parents enjoy peace of mind.

Occasional Care When You Need Us Most

Life doesn't always go as planned. When your regular childcare falls through, when school is closed but work isn't, when you just need a day, your Legend has a place here. Our Occasional Care program gives your child a safe, joyful day with us, no full-time enrollment required.

Flexible Backup Care

Book up to 7 days in advance, or same-day when space allows. Up to 9 hours of care per day, so your schedule stays on track no matter what comes up.

Everything Included

Meals and snacks are on us. We provide wipes and a full day of activities, learning, and play. Just bring diapers, a change of clothes, and anything special your child needs.

Same Love, No Commitment

Your child gets the same warm, safe, enrichment-filled experience as our enrolled Legends. A streamlined enrollment process means less paperwork and more peace of mind.
