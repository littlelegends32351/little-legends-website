Refer a Legend

Know a family looking for childcare? Send them our way. When they enroll and complete their first month, you earn $50. Enrolled families get a $50 tuition credit. Grandparents, friends, and neighbors get a $50 gift card. There's no limit to how many families you can refer. Just our way of saying thank you for helping our village grow.

Referrer must be named at the time of enrollment. Retroactive claims cannot be honored. The $50 reward is issued after the referred family completes their first full month of care. Enrolled families receive a $50 tuition credit. Community referrers receive a $50 gift card. No limit on referrals.
